For teachers & parents

Built for learners. Friendly to classrooms.

◆
CEFR A2–B1 core.

Past Simple and Past Continuous, questions, prepositions of time and place, polite requests — all through the story, not drills.

◆
Comprehensible input.

Short lines, clear context, and an optional Russian translation toggle for every page of dialogue.

◆
Feedback, not punishment.

Watson explains the mistake and the story moves on. Wrong answers cost nothing but teach something.

◆
Zero setup.

Runs in any modern browser. No account, no ads, no data collection. Progress saves automatically.

◆
One session, or many.

About an hour of play, chapter by chapter. Replay to chase the flawless badge run.

◆
Talk about it after.

The ending gives you a ready-made discussion: who had the motive, the means, the opportunity?
